Design Rationale

Why the dataset and question

We chose the amazon because although amazon is a household name very few people tend to think of the forest. Over the years it has grown smaller and smaller due to deforestation and left a last impact. This has seemingly because temperatures to raise as you can see from the data that may lead to more fires along with broken ecosystems due to the deforestation. If nothing else seeing this change happen in front of your own eyes will hopefully help other understand the amazon is more than just 2 day shipping.

Visual encoding

Satellite map is used just to show an actual picture however the land temp is what brings up the concern. In all honesty the average person may see the satellite map and think nothing of it but watching the change in temperatures when they are color coded for easy understanding helps get the point that there is an impact to deforestation much easier.

Interaction design

The slider is simple but effective as it gives the user to see the changes as they progress. This provides a heavier impact than a static frame or two. The toggle between the two visualizations helps connect the two in a deeper sense.

Alternatives

We did consider a side by side view but thought it would not be as impactful. Also initially wanted to do vegetation change but thought the heat map would be harder to hand wave. If there is less vegetation due to deforestation that's a bit of the point.
